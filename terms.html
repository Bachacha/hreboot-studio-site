Terms of Service

HReboot Studio · Effective November 14, 2026

1. Purpose

HReboot Studio is a desktop application for producing short vertical videos and publishing them to TikTok. It is operated by the creator of the TikTok channels listed on the home page (currently @h.reboot, @noor.coran.app, @noor.quran.inspir), for those channels. These terms describe how the studio is used with TikTok.

2. Use of TikTok

HReboot Studio connects to a TikTok account only with the explicit authorisation of the account holder, through TikTok's official sign-in. It sends videos prepared in the studio to that account, either as a direct post or as a draft in the TikTok app. Every upload is decided and confirmed manually: before a direct post, the creator chooses the visibility, the interactions allowed and the disclosure of any commercial content. A video made for one channel cannot be sent to another. Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Content

Direct posting is reserved for videos the creator has the rights to publish: original animated stories, explainers and data videos produced in the studio, and other authorised content. The creator confirms this before each direct post. Videos made with AI tools are labelled as AI-generated content.

Videos for the Noor channels use published Quran recitations and translations, credited to their sources; the Arabic text is never modified or generated.

Clips prepared from third-party videos are never posted directly: they can only be sent as drafts to the TikTok app.

In all cases the creator is solely responsible for the content published, for respecting copyright and for compliance with TikTok's Community Guidelines.

4. Availability

The studio is provided as is, without any guarantee of availability.

5. Withdrawing authorisation

HReboot Studio's access to a TikTok account can be withdrawn at any time, either with “Disconnect TikTok” in the studio or in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services).

6. Changes

These terms may be updated when the studio changes. The date at the top of this page is the date of the current version.

7. Contact
