The video studio behind the H-Reboot and Noor channels

HReboot Studio is a desktop application that takes a short vertical video from the first idea to the TikTok post: writing, images, animation, voices, editing, captions, cover and publishing, in one place.

How it works

Every video goes through the same stages. Nothing moves forward until the creator approves it.

1

Write

The script or story is written, then read and corrected by the creator before anything is produced.

2

Produce

Images, animation, voices and graphics are produced, checked and redone if needed.

3

Edit

Sound, captions and the cover frame are assembled into the final vertical video.

4

Review

The creator watches the finished video and edits the caption and hashtags.

5

Publish

The creator picks the visibility and interaction settings, then posts to TikTok or sends a draft.

6

Follow up

The studio shows TikTok's processing status until the video is live or in the drafts.

How HReboot Studio uses TikTok

The studio connects to TikTok through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only after the account holder has authorised it.

Sign in with TikTok

The creator clicks “Connect TikTok” and approves the request on TikTok's own page. The studio then shows the connected account's name and picture.

The creator decides every post

Before each post the creator chooses who can see the video, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, and discloses any commercial content. Nothing is posted without that confirmation.

Direct post or draft

Videos the creator has the rights to publish can be posted directly. Any video can instead be sent to the TikTok inbox as a draft, to finish in the TikTok app.

AI content is labelled

Videos made with AI image, video or voice tools are flagged as AI-generated content when they are posted.

Access stays on the computer

TikTok access tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on the creator's computer. They are never sent to another server.

Access can be withdrawn

The creator can disconnect in the studio, or remove the authorisation in the TikTok app under Security → Apps and services.

Questions

What is HReboot Studio?

It is the production and publishing studio of the H-Reboot and Noor TikTok channels: a desktop application operated by the creator of these channels.

Which TikTok accounts does it publish to?

Only the channels listed on this page, each connected by its owner through TikTok's official sign-in. A video made for one channel cannot be sent to another.

Does it post without the creator knowing?

No. Every post starts with an explicit click, after the creator has reviewed the video, the caption, the visibility and the interaction settings.

What data does it read from TikTok?

The basic profile of the connected account (identifier, display name, picture) and the posting options TikTok allows for it. It reads no viewer data. Details are in the privacy policy.

What about videos made from other people's content?

They are never posted directly. They can only be sent as drafts to the TikTok app, where the creator decides what to do with them, under the creator's responsibility and TikTok's rules.

Where do the Quran recitations and translations come from?

From published recitations and translations made available by Quran.com. The Arabic text is never modified or generated, and each source is credited.
