Privacy Policy

HReboot Studio · Effective November 14, 2026

1. Who this concerns

HReboot Studio is used by the creator of the TikTok channels listed on the home page (currently @h.reboot, @noor.coran.app, @noor.quran.inspir), for those accounts. It collects no data about viewers or any other TikTok user. This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

2. TikTok data used

With the account holder's authorisation, HReboot Studio accesses:

  • the account's basic information (identifier, display name, profile picture), to show which account is connected;
  • the account's posting options (available privacy levels, maximum video duration), which TikTok requires before any post;
  • the upload of videos to that account and the status of their processing.

It requests no other permission.

3. Storage

TikTok access tokens are stored only on the creator's computer, in the secure macOS Keychain, separately for each connected account. No data is sent to a third-party server, sold or shared.

4. Retention

Tokens are kept while the authorisation is active. They are deleted when the creator disconnects the account in HReboot Studio or removes the authorisation in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services).

5. Services used

To produce videos, HReboot Studio uses AI services for images, video and voice (only the texts and images of the videos are sent to them), a transcription service (ElevenLabs) and a text-analysis service (Claude, by Anthropic), which receive only the audio and transcripts of the videos being edited. Videos for the Noor channels use the public Quran.com service for the verse text, timings, recitations and translations. No TikTok data is ever sent to any of these services.

6. Your rights

The only personal data handled is that of the connected account holder, who can view it in the studio and delete it at any time by disconnecting the account. For any question or request, use the contact below.

7. Contact

By message on TikTok: @h.reboot.
